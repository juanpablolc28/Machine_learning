Use Case 02
Customer Segmentation for Marketing
Unsupervised Learning · Clustering
A machine learning system that analyzes customer data without predefined labels, automatically discovering groups of customers who share similar behaviors or preferences.
Problem Context
Companies want to design marketing campaigns tailored to different customer profiles, but manually classifying thousands of customers into meaningful groups is impractical.
Data Used
Purchase history, browsing behavior, age, location, and spending patterns.
Model Output
Clusters of customers who share similar purchasing behavior, without predefined categories.
Potential Benefit
More effective, personalized marketing campaigns and higher customer satisfaction.
