Types of Machine Learning

01

Supervised Learning

Definition: The model is trained on labeled data, learning to map inputs to known outputs.

Main characteristics

Requires labeled datasets; the correct answer is known during training; the model adjusts itself to minimize errors between its predictions and the real values.

Types of problems

Classification (e.g. spam detection) and regression (e.g. predicting a numeric value, like liters of gasoline from kilometers driven).

02

Unsupervised Learning

Definition: The model works with unlabeled data, identifying patterns and structures on its own.

Main characteristics

No labeled answers are provided; the model looks for hidden structure or similarity in the data by itself.

Types of problems

Clustering (grouping similar customers) and dimensionality reduction (simplifying data while keeping its structure).

03

Reinforcement Learning

Definition: The model learns by interacting with an environment, receiving rewards or penalties based on its actions.

Main characteristics

Learning happens through trial and error; there's an agent, an environment, and a reward signal guiding improvement over time.

Types of problems

Game playing, robotics control, and any scenario where an agent must make a sequence of decisions to maximize long-term reward.
